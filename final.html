01  Problem Statement

>> ERR_SUBJECTIVITY_NOT_FOUND

Artificial intelligence has become an omnipresent phenomenon, widely adopted and seamlessly integrated into daily life. Yet the internal logic of models often remains unseen. Public attention fixates on intelligence and efficiency, while few recognize what it means to become a data point under machine analysis.

This project investigates how human identity is transformed inside data-driven models. During dataset construction, individual images are abstracted into labels such as "Asian" / "female" / "smiling" / "tilted gaze". Through categorization, subjectivity is deconstructed, fragmented, and redefined by algorithms.

06  Concerns / Limitations / Ethical Notes

Entering the VOID. 

/!\ WARNING /!\
BIAS_DETECTED
> READ_PROTOCOL: ETHICS_V1.0

This project critically examines how AI systems break human identity into fragments, labels, and statistical categories.

Because we use the same computer-vision tools we aim to critique, we remain cautious about what these technologies reproduce. Our system does NOT attempt to classify people or store identifiable data; instead, all inputs are processed locally, with an intentionally limited and ethically constructed dataset.

We acknowledge the risks of bias, misrepresentation, and over-simplification inherent to machine perception. We treat these limitations not as features to optimize, but as conditions to make visible.
